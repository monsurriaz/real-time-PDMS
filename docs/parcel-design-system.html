Why in transit is the brand orange. The accent isn't decoration sitting next to the statuses — it is one of them. The product's whole reason to exist is the moving parcel, so whenever something is moving, the brand color is on screen. When nothing is moving, the interface goes quiet. Failed and Cancelled sit off the ramp as exits, not stages.
